ACADEMIC STREAMS

ARTS

Humanities establishes the groundwork for the genesis of a stable, ordered and progressive society. We host numerous activities to pave the way for the students in becoming aware social beings. Seminars, workshops, public speaking, quiz are regularly held for the students.

SCIENCE

Science literally means knowledge. Science is the study of the nature and behavior of natural things and the knowledge that we obtain about them. We include exhibitions, science quiz, laboratory practicals and field visits to enable our students to excell in the subjects.

COMMERCE

Commerce being the core of economic development needs a deep understanding of every aspect of the subject. Good tutors in combination with seminars, surveys and projects have been delineating favorable results for the students.
